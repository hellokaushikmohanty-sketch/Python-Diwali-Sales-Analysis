# 🪔 Python Diwali Sales Analysis

![Diwali Sales Analysis](Diwali_Sales_Analysis_Consumer_Profile.png)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes Diwali sales data using **Python** to identify customer purchasing patterns, high-value customer segments, regional trends, occupational influences, and product preferences.

The project goes beyond basic exploratory data analysis by combining **data visualization, business interpretation, data storytelling, and AI-assisted documentation**.

The main objective is to answer:

> **Who are the most valuable Diwali customers, where are they located, what do they do, and what products are they most likely to purchase?**

---

## 🎯 Business Questions

The analysis focuses on the following questions:

- Who contributes the most to Diwali sales — male or female customers?
- Which age group has the highest purchasing activity?
- Which states generate the highest sales?
- Which occupations contribute the most to purchasing power?
- Does marital status influence purchasing behavior?
- Which product categories are most frequently purchased?
- Which products generate the highest number of orders?
- What does the overall high-value customer profile look like?

# 🔎 Analysis Performed

## 1. Gender Analysis

Customer volume and total purchasing amount were compared by gender.

### Key Insight

Female customers represent the larger customer segment and demonstrate substantially higher purchasing power in the analyzed dataset.

This indicates that female consumers are an important target segment for Diwali marketing strategies.

---

## 2. Age Group Analysis

Customers were segmented into different age groups to identify the strongest purchasing demographic.

### Key Insight

The **26–35 age group** emerged as the strongest demographic, leading in both transaction activity and total expenditure.

The analysis also identifies a strong intersection between:

**Female + 26–35 years**

making this an important customer segment.

---

## 3. Geographic Analysis

Customer purchasing behavior was analyzed across different states.

### Top Performing States

1. **Uttar Pradesh**
2. **Maharashtra**
3. **Karnataka**

Uttar Pradesh leads in both orders and total amount, while Karnataka presents an interesting relationship between order volume and spending.

This can help businesses prioritize regional marketing, inventory, and distribution strategies.

---

## 4. Occupation Analysis

Customer purchasing behavior was analyzed across professional sectors.

### Leading Occupations

- IT
- Healthcare
- Aviation
- Banking

The **IT sector** emerged as the strongest occupational segment in the analysis.

---

## 5. Marital Status Analysis

Marital status was analyzed alongside gender to understand differences in purchasing behavior.

### Key Insight

The analysis indicates that **married women represent a particularly strong high-value customer segment**.

This may indicate that household-oriented and family-related purchasing plays an important role during the Diwali sales period.

---

## 6. Product Category Analysis

Product categories were compared using both purchasing frequency and total purchasing amount.

### Major Categories

- 👗 Clothing & Apparel
- 🍱 Food
- 💻 Electronics & Gadgets

An important data-literacy insight is that:

> **The category with the highest purchase frequency is not necessarily the category generating the highest total spending.**

This distinction is important when making inventory and marketing decisions.

---

## 7. Product-Level Analysis

Individual products were analyzed to identify high-volume products.

Two notable products identified during the analysis include:

```text
P00256242
P00110942
```

These products demonstrated particularly high order volumes and could be considered for inventory and restocking attention.

---

# 🧠 High-Value Customer Profile

By combining the demographic, geographic, professional, and product-level findings, the analysis identifies the following high-value customer profile:

> **A married woman aged 26–35, located in Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, or Karnataka, working in sectors such as IT, Healthcare, or Aviation, with strong purchasing interest in Food, Clothing & Apparel, and Electronics & Gadgets.**

This demonstrates the value of **intersectional customer analysis**.

Instead of targeting a broad audience such as:

> "Diwali shoppers"

businesses can develop more focused segments such as:

> **26–35-year-old female customers working in the IT sector.**

# 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses could consider the following strategies:

### 1. Target the 26–35 Female Segment

Develop focused marketing campaigns around the strongest demographic segment.

### 2. Prioritize High-Performing States

Focus regional campaigns, inventory planning, and distribution efforts on:

- Uttar Pradesh
- Maharashtra
- Karnataka

### 3. Develop Profession-Based Campaigns

Consider targeted campaigns for high-performing occupational segments such as:

- IT
- Healthcare
- Aviation

### 4. Optimize Inventory

Monitor high-volume products and maintain adequate inventory before major Diwali sales periods.

### 5. Use Intersectional Targeting

Combine multiple attributes such as:

**Age + Gender + Occupation + Geography**

to create more precise customer segments.
